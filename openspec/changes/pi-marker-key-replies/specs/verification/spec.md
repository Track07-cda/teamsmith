## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Marker reply evidence exercises product capture and real Pi boundaries

Marker-capture verification SHALL use the product parser and existing real writer for syntax, complete content/offset equality, raw-body fidelity, source identity and writer outcomes. A copied regex, fake receipt or document-presence check MUST NOT stand in for those behaviours. The existing reply guide judgement SHALL be replaced to exercise marked default replies and manual/backfill compatibility through the same reply gate entry; this change MUST NOT add an independent process/health guard. Existing UI/pty, paging, atomic visibility, receipt and read-only coverage SHALL remain effective. Any new test drivers and fault scaffolding MUST live in tests, not product conditionals.

A private real Pi with a controlled loopback model SHALL independently demonstrate end/binding/settled ordering, final transcript/print/history replacement, actual loading/reload and duplicate copies, a queued continuation, terminal-state cleanup and interrupted prefixes. Raw pty matching MUST use the marker actually emitted by each case with a positive selector control; sampled frames MAY supplement but MUST NOT replace raw evidence or claim no flash from a negative sample. No fixed flash deadline or real-model compliance claim SHALL be inferred. Fake-host tests MAY exercise state transitions/fault permutations but MUST NOT be presented as real rendering, persistence or provider-history proof. Neither planning nor automated evidence authorizes live-team init/reload/restart/testing.

The evidence SHALL include real busy and unconfirmed writer paths; crash/fault boundaries at staging, Pi append, binding, eligibility, attempting, spawn, writer commit/receipt and outcome recording; duplicate events and copies; same-body distinct sources; saved sources outside the newest page; uncertain outcomes with no automatic reattempt; session/branch/fork switching; foreign cwd plus inherited root/config/state; disk-versus-loaded diagnostics; and private project snapshots showing no publication-created BOARD/inbox/outbox/pending/wake effects. Review SHALL remain by a different agent than implementation. Targeted mutations MUST demonstrably break their behavioural assertions and restoration MUST recover green.

#### Scenario: Escaped suffix parsing has a real red side
- **WHEN** product-parser tests run the escaped-first-tick inline-only and inline-plus-real-control cases, adjacent odd/even-backslash controls and an in-span backslash-before-closer case
- **THEN** exact content and real-writer count/raw-body assertions pass for the correct parser
- **AND** ignoring an entire physical tick run because its first tick is escaped turns the suffix cases red; ignoring parity or in-span closer rules turns their controls red; restoring code restores green

#### Scenario: The evidence cannot be replaced by stubs
- **WHEN** verification reports busy, unconfirmed, final rendering/history or duplicate-load success
- **THEN** busy came from holding the real writer lock, unconfirmed from the existing real-writer fault path, and rendering/history/loading came from private real Pi artifacts
- **AND** replacing those observations by fake receipts/hosts is explicitly insufficient, with no live-team state used as a test fixture

#### Scenario: Source ownership and settlement assertions are falsifiable
- **WHEN** source-attempt serialization/owner checks or the final-settlement/latest-message predicate are removed in disposable mutations
- **THEN** duplicate-load/concurrent-source or queued/abort-supersession assertions respectively fail, and a pre-attempt crash record cannot be silently promoted from pending
- **AND** restored product code passes, with journal/raw-store evidence and no hash/current-page reconciliation

#### Scenario: Marker selection and no-work effects are independently inspected
- **WHEN** private Pi emits the actual complete marker in pieces, a protected example, an aborted prefix and a final eligible answer
- **THEN** raw logs show the known streamed-marker positive and the cleaned final states or documented literal-prefix boundary, without a timing threshold
- **AND** private task/inbox/outbox/notification/pending snapshots remain unchanged by publication, while legitimate runtime/journal/reply artifacts are identified separately
