## Findings that must be closed in the exploration

### F1 · Profile A does not cover the changed-path case that just failed in public CI

The report leaves “changed paths unchanged” and reserves about ten minutes for one to six added sections. That is not a viable worst-case push/PR profile: a routing-fixture change selects §61b even though §61b is outside the floor.

Public run `40433391806` is the direct counterexample. Its coverage log lists §61b in shard 3's 33 keys; shard 3 entered §61b at 01:55:24Z, was still emitting progress at 02:34:25Z (2341s), and was cancelled at 02:35:01Z at the 60-minute job ceiling. The run also selected the ledger section from `docs/team/**` changes. Therefore “outside the floor” does not mean “outside an ordinary push”.

Required revision: give a selection-and-cost matrix for at least docs-only, ordinary product, routing fixture (`task-model-routing.sh` / `model-routing-flips.py`), and selector-self-check changes. State what happens to §61b and §36 in each profile **both when they are in the floor and when changed paths select them**. Recompute the worst shard, not only the floor. Do not approve a ≤30-minute claim while one allowed input adds a 6719-second section unchanged.

Evidence: `$HOME/.cache/release034/run-40433391806-job-121956221892.log`, `$HOME/.cache/release034/run-40433391806-job-121975547545.log`, and the release diff's `skills/teamsmith/tests/task-model-routing.sh` path.

### F2 · The nightly §61b arithmetic does not support the stated 15 minutes

The report assigns approximately 2518 seconds to 24 cheaper arms and puts them in two jobs. At the reported current costs that is at least 1259 seconds = **21.0 minutes per job**, before setup and the green baseline, not “§61b job ≈15 min”. Seven boundary jobs plus two cheaper-arm jobs plus four ordinary shards also means 13 jobs, not a four-job shape.

Required revision: show the job count and wall-time arithmetic explicitly, including the green baseline and setup. If the 15-minute figure assumes filtering each arm to named assertions, say that it is a different coverage trade-off and provide a small measured prototype or keep it as an unmeasured estimate. Distinguish “the current complete matrix” from “all named assertions, with incidental mutation breadth removed”; do not call both complete coverage.

### F3 · Same-host isolation is stated too broadly

The report first says process-view sections must not be co-scheduled because they read `/proc`, then says separate containers with `--pid=host` are fine. On one host, those containers still share the host process view; gate-lock paths and a tree fingerprint can also be shared unless their roots are separated. GitHub's separate runners are a different isolation case.

Required revision: separate (a) independent CI jobs on separate runners from (b) two containers on one host. For (b), identify which sections are unsafe with `--pid=host`, shared `/tmp`, a shared gate lock, or a shared tree. Do not treat a temp root alone as proof that every section can run concurrently.

### F4 · The disputed §38/§63 numbers and the real §63 red remain approval boundaries

The report already establishes that the §38/§63 time sources disagree and that the `mouse` assertion is red in both host and pinned container. Those facts are not a license to freeze the 20-minute profile as accepted arithmetic.

Required revision: keep the affected projections explicitly provisional until one pinned-container composition measurement exists. The §63 red needs its own defect task and root cause; it must not be “fixed” by moving that leg to nightly or by weakening the assertion. The mislabelled `62` messages inside §63 may be noted separately but are not the defect's root cause.
